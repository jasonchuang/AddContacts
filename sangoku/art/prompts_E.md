# 武將頭像提示詞（E 批）

延續目前頭像的風格：日系動畫賽璐璐上色、半寫實比例、華麗甲冑或文官袍服、半身像、背景是天空或城樓。
每組 6 人排成 2 列 × 3 欄。生成後直接把整張圖傳給 Claude，說明哪一格是誰（有不滿意的格子可以說「跳過」）。

**使用方式**：每組都先貼「共用風格」，再貼該組的內容。
- ChatGPT 或 Gemini：整段直接貼。
- Midjourney：在最後加上 `--ar 6:5`（整張 2×3）或 `--ar 4:5`（單人）。

## 共用風格（每次都貼在最前面）

```
Anime cel-shaded illustration, semi-realistic proportions, ancient Chinese Han dynasty setting.
Half-body portrait, three-quarter view, face clearly visible in the upper 40% of each panel, sharp clean line art,
rich detailed costume (ornate lamellar armor with gold trim for warriors, layered silk robes for scholars),
soft cinematic lighting. Each panel is a separate portrait with its own background.
No text, no names, no labels, no watermark, no borders between panels wider than a thin line.
```

## 單人版：郭嘉（黑暗軍師）

```
Anime cel-shaded illustration, semi-realistic proportions, ancient Chinese Han dynasty setting.
Half-body portrait of a brilliant but sickly young military strategist in his thirties, the "dark advisor" of a warlord.
Pale skin, handsome gaunt face, faint dark circles under sharp narrow eyes that seem to see through everything,

Layered robes of black and deep indigo silk with dark silver cloud embroidery, a black fur-trimmed cloak.
He raises a small bronze wine cup in one hand; the other hand rests on a go board with black stones surrounding white.
A faint red stain on a white handkerchief tucked in his sleeve hints at his illness.
Night scene: cold blue moonlight, warm candlelight from inside a war tent, a war map on the table,
dark ink-like smoke curling around him, a few crows on a dead branch.
Mysterious, dangerous, elegant, tragic. Face in the upper 40% of the image, three-quarter view.
No text, no watermark.
```

## 生成小技巧

- 6 人一張有時候會把兩個人畫得很像。若某一格不滿意，用單人版重畫那一個就好：拿掉 grid 那一行，只貼該格描述。
- 若人物年紀或胖瘦跟描述差太多，在那一格開頭加 `IMPORTANT:`。
- 勢力主色：曹魏深藍、蜀漢綠、孫吳紅，袁紹金黃、董卓紫黑。顏色差太多時可以提醒生成工具。
